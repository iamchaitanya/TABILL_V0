Privacy Policy for TA BILL

Last updated: 25 November 2026

TA BILL ("the app") is a tour allowance and inspection diary tool for official use. This policy explains what the app stores and who can see it.

1. Information the app stores

  • Profile details: name, employee ID, contact details, address, designation, pay and bank-account fields you choose to enter.
  • Inspection data: tour dates, branches, DP codes, journey and expense records you save.
  • Service credentials: the CBS, DMS, HRMS and ELEARNING login details you enter in the "Passwords" section.
  • Photos: a profile avatar you upload (resized on your device before storage).
  • Google account information: if you sign in with Google, the app receives your email address, name and profile picture from Google.

2. Where the data lives

The app works offline: your profile and inspection records are stored locally in your browser. If you sign in with Google, the same data is synchronised to a private account in Supabase (our database provider) so it is available on your other devices. The app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages.

Every database row belongs to your Google account and is protected by Row Level Security: only you can read or write your own rows. We use a public "publishable" key in the app; there is no secret key in the browser build.

3. About your credentials

The CBS / DMS / HRMS / ELEARNING details are stored as plain JSON inside your own profile row so they can follow your account between devices. They are protected by the same per-account access controls as the rest of your data and are not shared with other users, but they are not additionally encrypted at rest. Do not use this section for credentials whose exposure would be serious, and consider using a separate, limited-access account for these services.

4. How we use your information

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising. It is used only to provide the app: saving your records, generating reports, and syncing your own data across your devices.

5. Sharing

We do not share your data with other users or third parties, except the service providers needed to run the app: Google (sign-in), Supabase (database and authentication) and Cloudflare (hosting).

6. Your choices and deletion

Signing in is optional; without it the app remains fully usable and your data stays only in this browser. You can sign out at any time from the Profile page. Clearing your browser's site data removes the local copy. To have your cloud account and its data deleted, contact the developer using the address below.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or want your data removed, please contact the developer of TA BILL.
