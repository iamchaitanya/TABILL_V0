Delete your TA BILL account and data

To have your TA BILL account and its cloud data deleted, email contact@frontierstride.com with the subject "Delete TA BILL account" and include the Google account email you used to sign in.

What we delete

Within 7 days we permanently delete your profile (including your name, contact and bank/pay details, and the CBS/DMS/HRMS/ELEARNING logins), your tour records, and all of your inspection entries from our database.

What is kept

No personal data is kept after deletion, except where retention is required by law. Deleted rows cannot be recovered.

Data on your device

Sign out in the app's Profile page, then clear this website's storage in your browser (Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Site settings → TA BILL → Delete data) to remove the local copy. Uninstalling the Android app also removes its local data.
